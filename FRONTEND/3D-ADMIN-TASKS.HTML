Who are admins

SIMS admins are the Leads (GL / SGL in PIMS) and Material Leads of the IPMCS team of any job / unit. They can delegate 3D admin tasks, mark any task done and backfill missing tasks. To change who is admin, change the team in IPMCS (Team page) — SIMS keeps no separate list.
